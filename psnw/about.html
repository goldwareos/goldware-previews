About Dr. Smith

F. Ames Smith Jr. MD

30+ years as a breast cancer surgeon, now dedicated to hereditary cancer risk.

Board certifiedAmerican Board of Surgery

A surgeon's experience, focused on prevention

Dr. Smith has dedicated his practice exclusively to managing men and women with high risk for cancer, especially those with an inherited (genetic) predisposition. His decades of general and breast surgical experience uniquely position him to lead this work.

He earned his medical degree from the University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston in 1984 and completed surgical training at Baylor College of Medicine in Houston in 1990. He practiced in Austin from 1990 to 2012, then was recruited to Seattle as Director of Breast Surgery for The Polyclinic, where he established and led the Polyclinic Hereditary Cancer Risk Clinic through 2022.

In 2019 he completed intensive cancer genetics education through City of Hope, and he is part of its Clinical Cancer Genomics Community of Practice. After moving to North Idaho in 2022 he founded Physician Specialists Northwest. He is a member of the Independent Doctors of Idaho, works collaboratively with local and regional providers, and sees patients in the office and by telehealth.
