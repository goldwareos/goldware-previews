What happens next

  1. The office contacts you to find a time in Spokane, Sandpoint, Boise or by telehealth.
  2. Upload your driver's license and insurance cards in the patient portal.
  3. Gather your family's cancer history: who had cancer, what type and at what age.
  4. Your visit with Dr. Smith takes about 45 minutes.
